# monthly-revenue-case-study
SQL-based analysis of monthly revenue performance from January to June 2026 using MySQL, including revenue trends, month-to-month changes, rankings, and business recommendations.
# Monthly Revenue Case Study

## Project Overview
This project analyses monthly revenue performance from January to June 2026 using MySQL. The goal is to identify revenue trends, compare monthly performance, and develop data-driven recommendations for further investigation.

## Business Questions
- What were the total and average monthly revenues?
- Which months recorded the highest and lowest revenue?
- Which months experienced revenue increases or decreases?
- How did monthly revenue changes compare in absolute and percentage terms?
- Which months performed below the average revenue?
- How can revenue rankings help compare monthly performance?

## Tools Used
- MySQL
- MySQL Workbench

## Key Findings
- **Total revenue:** ₦960,000
- **Average monthly revenue:** ₦160,000
- **Highest revenue:** June 2026, ₦210,000
- **Lowest revenue:** January 2026, ₦120,000
- **Largest monthly increases:** April and June, ₦45,000 each
- **Largest monthly decreases:** March and May, ₦15,000 each
- **Months below average:** January, February, and March

March recorded a 10% decrease compared with February, while May recorded an approximately 8.3% decrease compared with April.

## Recommendations
Investigate marketing performance, pricing, sales volume, product quality, and seasonal demand to better understand the observed revenue fluctuations.

## Limitations
The dataset contains monthly revenue figures only. It identifies patterns but does not establish why revenue increased or decreased. Additional business data would be required to determine the underlying causes.
